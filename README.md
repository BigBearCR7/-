中国进入工业化后期，在产业资本的推动下向金融资本主导的全球化竞争的新阶段跃升。
China has entered the late stage of industrialization, and driven by industrial capital, has leapt into a new stage of global competition dominated by financial capital.
发展中国家（developing country）进入工业化需要资本，需要按照国外资本的制度要求改造本国意识形态进而影响上层建筑和政策制度。
Developing countries need capital to enter industrialization, and must transform their own ideologies according to the institutional requirements of foreign capital, thereby influencing the superstructure and policy systems.
中国在发展工业化的过程中，引入资本带来的政策转变进而使危机频频发生。
During China's industrialization development, policy changes brought by the introduction of capital have led to frequent crises.
但想维护国家主权独立必须完成工业化，在城市促进工商业，在农村发展中农经济保留富农经济，形成城乡经济循环，从而积累资本。
However, to safeguard national sovereignty and independence, industrialization must be completed, promoting industry and commerce in cities, retaining the rich peasant economy in rural development, forming an urban-rural economic cycle, and thus accumulating capital.
在工业化进程中，经济周期性波动是无法避免的。
During industrialization, cyclical economic fluctuations are unavoidable.
人类文明进入资本主义阶段，所有民族被核心国家整合到同一个世界经济体系，形成“核心—半边缘—边缘”的结构，由于不平等的交换，资源向核心输送，此消彼长，强者恒强。
Human civilization has entered the capitalist stage, with all ethnic groups integrated into the core state into a single world economic system, forming a "core—semi-periphery"—periphery structure. Due to unequal exchange, resources flow to the core, with one side gaining the other, and the strong growing stronger.
